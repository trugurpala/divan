# Divan Governance

## Decision authority

The repository owner sets product direction, security boundaries and release
authority. Maintainers may merge work only inside their documented scope and
after required evidence is green. Contributors propose and implement changes;
popularity, employment, or tool access does not expand authority.

## Contributor path

1. Use [SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md) to choose the right public or private route.
2. Discuss a material product direction before implementation.
3. Open a focused change with tests, provenance and rollback notes.
4. Address independent review and required CI findings.
5. A maintainer merges; the release workflow alone creates a public release.

## Maintainer responsibilities

Maintainers preserve the one-repository modular architecture, review licenses
and security boundaries, keep Turkish and English public meaning aligned, and
verify the default branch after merge. No maintainer may call a draft pull
request or an untagged commit a release.
